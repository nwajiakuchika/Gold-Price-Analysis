**GOLD PRICE ANALYSIS AND FORECASTING**

**Overview**

This project explore historical gold price data to analyze market trends,
price movements,and volatility over time.The dataset include keys financial
indicators and technicasl metrics that helps understand gold market behavior
and support forecasting analysis.

**Dataset Features**
- Date
- Open Price
- High Price
- Low PRICE
- Close Price
- Adjust Close Price
- Trading Volume
- 7-Day MovING Average (MA-7)
- 30-Day Moving Average (MA-30)
- 90-Day Moving Average (MA-90)
- Daily Return
- 7-Day Volatility
- 30-Day Volatility

- **Objective**
- Analyze historical gold price trends
- Examines market volatility and daily returns
- Explore relationship between price movements and trading volume
- Evaluate moving average as indicator of market direction
- Generates insights that support gold price forecasting

- **Tools Used**
- Python,Pandas,Matplotlib,Numpy,Seaborn,and Jupiter NoteBook

- **Key Insight**
- Gold prices exhibit changing market trends influenced by volatility
- and trading activity.moving average and return metrics provide valuable
- indicators for understanding market performance and forecasting future
- price behavior.

- **Conclusion**

- This analysis demostrates how financial data can betransform into meanifull
- insight through exploratory data analysis and visualization.The findings serve
- as a foundation for predictive modeling and investment focused decision making.
